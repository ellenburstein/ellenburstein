02 / About

I'm a writer and social researcher interested in how institutions shape intimate and everyday experiences. My work draws on anthropology, migration studies, and reporting to explore questions of health, law, and belonging.

I studied social anthropology at Harvard and medical anthropology and migration studies at the University of Oxford.
